## 1. Editorial Voice & Anti-AI-Slop Invariants (`hallmark` + `taste-skill`)

Every word published under the FRAMIQUE domain must feel human-crafted, authoritative, and grounded in quantifiable commercial and technical reality.

### The 5 Anti-Slop Non-Negotiables:
1. **Never use generic LLM filler openings:**
   - *Forbidden:* "In today's fast-paced digital world...", "In the ever-evolving landscape of ecommerce...", "Are you tired of...", "Look no further!"
   - *Required:* Open directly with a concrete economic friction, technical mechanism, or empirical metric.
2. **Never use inflated synthetic superlatives:**
   - *Forbidden:* "Revolutionary", "game-changing", "cutting-edge", "unleash", "seamlessly integrate", "supercharge", "robust".
   - *Required:* Describe the exact mechanical action: "Evaluates zero-fee checkouts", "renders sub-300ms pre-compiled HTML", "tokenizes bKash/Nagad APIs directly".
3. **High-Contrast Rhythm & Varied Sentence Length:**
   - Alternate between short, declarative punches (3–6 words) and detailed architectural explanations.
4. **Concrete Nouns Over Abstract Buzzwords:**
   - Instead of "optimize your conversion funnel", write: "eliminate the 2.0% third-party gateway penalty and 3-second mobile lag that causes 38% cart abandonment."
5. **Radical Architectural Honesty:**
   - Clearly delineate where FRAMIQUE shines (custom design, 0% fees, high-speed SSR, regional rails) and acknowledge when a simple marketplace like Amazon or an off-the-shelf basic store is sufficient.
