## 4. Behavioral Price Psychology Framing (sickn33 Strategy)

Applying `sickn33-price-psychology-strategist` principles to keyword content:

1. **Anchoring Against the Shopify Tax:**  
   Always anchor the merchant against Shopify’s $39/mo base + 2% transaction fee + $150/mo app stack ($2,700+/year minimum). Framique's flat pricing eliminates fee anxiety.
2. **Payment Decoupling:**  
   In Framique, the customer connects directly with the merchant's payment gateway. The merchant collects 100% of the gross sale directly into their business bank account or MFS wallet with zero platform intermediary delays.
3. **Loss Aversion Trigger:**  
   Framing: *"How much did Shopify's 2% fee cost your brand last month? Enter your monthly GMV into the Framique Margin Reclaim Calculator."*
