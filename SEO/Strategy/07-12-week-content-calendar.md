## 3. Weekly Production & Distribution Workflow

Every single piece of content follows the **FLOW Verification Pipeline**:

```
[ MONDAY: RESEARCH ]   ──► Extract SERP intent, competitors & PAA queries via Semrush/Search
          │
          ▼
[ TUESDAY: DRAFTING ]  ──► Write semantic draft with strict H1->H2->H3 tree & S-P-O triples
          │
          ▼
[ WEDNESDAY: REVIEW ]  ──► Validate against Koray Tuğberk Gübür Quality Gate (Information Gain)
          │
          ▼
[ THURSDAY: PUBLISH ]  ──► Deploy to TanStack Start route + inject JSON-LD Schema markup
          │
          ▼
[ FRIDAY: COMMUNITY ]  ──► Seed discussion on Reddit (r/ecommerce, r/webdev, r/framer)
```

## 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & Review Cadence

- **Organic Search Velocity:** Number of top-10 ranking keywords across US, UK, and BD tracked weekly via Semrush.
- **AI Overview Ingestion Rate:** Frequency of Framique citations in Google Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude for target competitor queries.
- **Conversion Rate to Registration:** Percentage of organic blog readers who click through to `/auth?mode=signup`.
- **Monthly Review Date:** Last Friday of each calendar month to re-calibrate keywords based on seasonal search shifts.
