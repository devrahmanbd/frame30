## 3. Wireframe & Section Structure per Page Type

### 3.1 Head-to-Head Comparison Page Wireframe (`/compare/*`)
1. **Hero Section**:
   - Single semantic `<h1>` targeting `[Brand A] vs [Brand B]`.
   - Punchy, high-contrast subheadline derived from validated tagline.
   - Primary CTA (`"Start Free Store"`) + Secondary CTA (`"Calculate Savings"`).
2. **Interactive Proof Component**:
   - Dynamic slider or toggle proving the financial/operational claim.
3. **Structured Comparison Table**:
   - Clear feature-by-feature matrix with green/red/amber status markers.
4. **Deep-Dive Architecture Teardown**:
   - 3 modular feature spotlight cards detailing engineering advantages.
5. **Migration & Onboarding Workflow**:
   - 3-step visual guide demonstrating zero-downtime transition.
6. **Bilingual FAQ Section with JSON-LD Schema**:
   - Addressing key customer objections (migration time, custom domains, pricing stability).
7. **Bottom Conversion Band**:
   - Final risk-reversal CTA with social proof markers.
