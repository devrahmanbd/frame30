### Strategic Keyword Moat
1. **Shopify's Vulnerability:** High recurring USD cost ($39–$399/mo), 2.0% transaction fee penalty on 3rd-party gateways, absence of native local mobile financial service (MFS) checkouts (bKash, Nagad), and slow TTFB for regional consumers.
2. **Webflow's Vulnerability:** E-commerce is secondary, capped CMS items, expensive tiered checkouts, no native local courier logistics automation.
3. **Framer's Vulnerability:** Primarily a marketing site and portfolio tool; requires Frankenstein integrations (Lemon Squeezy, Shopify Buy Buttons) with zero native order lifecycle or inventory matrix.
4. **Framique's Wedge:** "Design freedom of Framer, transactional muscle of Shopify, zero platform fees, and native local payment + courier automation."
