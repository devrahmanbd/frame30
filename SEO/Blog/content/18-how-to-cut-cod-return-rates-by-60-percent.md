## 1. The Real Cost of Return-to-Origin (RTO)

In markets where Cash-on-Delivery (COD) represents 60% to 75% of e-commerce transactions, high return rates (RTO) are the single biggest threat to merchant solvency.

Many store owners only calculate the outbound delivery fee (e.g., 60–100 BDT). In reality, the true financial cost of a rejected parcel is **three times higher**:

$$\text{Total RTO Cost} = \text{Outbound Delivery Fee} + \text{Return Delivery Fee (50\%)} + \text{Packaging Depreciation} + \text{Dead Inventory Holding Cost}$$

On an average order of 1,500 BDT:
- Outbound courier fee: 100 BDT
- Return courier fee: 50 BDT
- Damaged packaging & labeling: 30 BDT
- **Total Direct Loss Per Returned Parcel:** **180 BDT**

If a merchant ships 1,000 parcels per month with a 25% return rate, they lose **45,000 BDT ($375 USD) every month** purely on non-delivery fees.

---

## 2. The 3 Root Causes of COD Rejections

1. **Impulsive Duplicate Ordering:** Shoppers order the same shirt or gadget from three different Facebook pages simultaneously and accept whichever parcel arrives at their door first.
2. **Fake or Mistyped Mobile Numbers:** Delivery riders cannot reach the buyer via phone to coordinate parcel drop-off.
3. **Buyer's Remorse During Slow Delivery:** If delivery takes more than 72 hours, the buyer loses emotional interest in the purchase.
