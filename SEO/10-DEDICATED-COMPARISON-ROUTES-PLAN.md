## 1. Executive Summary & Intent Strategy

In modern search and generative engine optimization (AEO/GEO), users searching for comparison terms (`"X vs Y"`, `"X alternatives"`) are at the **most aware** and **solution/product evaluation** stages (Eugene Schwartz Awareness Ladder / `sickn33-awareness-stage-mapper`). They have already recognized the problem (expensive transaction fees, rigid templates, CMS caps, broken plugin updates) and are evaluating feature architectures, total cost of ownership (TCO), and performance.

Competitors dominate isolated niches:
1. **Shopify** dominates high-volume catalog commerce, but locks merchants into a **2.0% third-party gateway penalty**, costly app subscriptions, and rigid templates.
2. **Webflow** provides visual design precision, but enforces a strict **2,000 CMS item ceiling** and suffers from sluggish client-side JavaScript execution.
3. **Framer** offers a brilliant Figma-like canvas, but lacks **native inventory management, transactional checkouts, or localized payment rails**.
4. **WooCommerce** offers self-hosted freedom, but burdens merchants with constant **plugin conflicts, security vulnerabilities, and database bloat**.

FRAMIQUE captures the sweet spot: **A SaaS Cloud CMS uniting freeform visual design with native high-speed commerce, 0% transaction fee sovereignty, and direct local payment rails**.
