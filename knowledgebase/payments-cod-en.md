**Q: How can I pay for my order?**

We accept bKash, Nagad, Rocket, and Upay mobile banking, Visa and Mastercard debit and credit cards through a card acquirer, bank transfer, and Cash on Delivery (COD) — all in one checkout. Every transaction is encrypted and reconciled against the order that produced it.

**Answer**

- Mobile banking: pay directly with bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or Upay from your own wallet or account.
- Cards: Visa and Mastercard debit and credit cards via the acquirer's hosted flow — card details go to the acquirer and never reach store servers.
- Bank transfer: follow the account details shown at checkout and keep the transaction reference.
- Cash on Delivery: pay in cash when the courier hands over the parcel; available across Bangladesh.
- After payment, the order ledger records the transaction reference, so support can trace any payment with your order number.

**Conditions**

- Online mobile-banking and card payments need an active wallet or card with sufficient balance or limit; failed authorizations leave the order unpaid, not cancelled — you can retry from the order page.
- COD orders may be held for a confirmation call if the order is risk-flagged (high value, new address, or prior non-receipt history).
- Partial payments across two methods on a single order are not supported; pick one method per order.
- Refunds always go back through the original rail or to the stated account; they are never issued as store credit unless you explicitly choose it.
