**Q: How long does delivery take, and what does it cost?**

We deliver to all 64 districts of Bangladesh using SteadFast Courier, Pathao Logistics, RedX, and Paperfly — booked, labelled, and tracked from inside the same order system, so there is no second portal and no copy-pasted address.

**Answer**

- Inside Dhaka: typically 1–2 business days.
- Outside Dhaka (anywhere in Bangladesh): typically 2–4 business days.
- Delivery charges are calculated from the destination and the package weight, and the exact charge is shown clearly at checkout before you pay.
- Cash on Delivery (COD) is available across Bangladesh.

**Conditions**

- Timings are estimates for normal operating days; public holidays, strikes, floods, or courier-side disruptions can add days.
- The charge shown at checkout is final for that order — if a courier rate changes later, it does not apply retroactively to confirmed orders.
- Remote or hard-to-reach areas may fall at the upper end of the estimate; the courier confirms the delivery date by phone or SMS where needed.
- If your parcel is delayed beyond the estimate, contact support with your order number and we trace it with the courier.
