**Q: How is Framique priced, and do you take a commission on sales?**

A flat monthly plan fee in BDT, published on the pricing page. Plans differ by staff seats, stores, and feature depth — not by how much you sell. There is strictly 0% per-transaction commission on all plans.

**Answer**

- 14-day free trial: full platform access with no card required; the trial never converts to a paid plan on its own.
- Starter: monthly or yearly billing; up to 500 products, free SSL, bKash and Nagad checkout, SteadFast integration, standard support.
- Growth: monthly or yearly billing; unlimited products, custom domain, Pathao and RedX couriers, bulk label printing, advanced analytics, priority live-chat support.
- Scale/Enterprise: monthly or yearly billing; multi-staff RBAC, dedicated account manager, custom API integrations, surge capacity, and a 99.99% uptime SLA.
- All plans include managed hosting, daily backups, DDoS protection, and responsive support.

**Conditions**

- Exact current figures live on the pricing page — confirm there before quoting; plan contents above follow the platform reference and may trail the latest packaging.
- No per-order fee at any tier: a good sales month does not enlarge the invoice. You move up only when you need more seats, stores, or a specific capability.
- Payment providers and couriers charge their own published rates directly to you; Framique does not mark them up.
- Cancel any time from the billing screen with no notice period and no cancellation fee; data stays exportable through the end of the paid period.
- Every payment produces a dated VAT invoice with your business name and BIN (where supplied), downloadable as PDF from the billing screen.
