**Q: How do I track my order after it ships?**

Every dispatched parcel gets a courier tracking (AWB/consignment) code when the merchant books the shipment. Status changes — booked, in transit, out for delivery, delivered, cancelled, returned — sync back automatically and are visible on your customer dashboard tracking view.

**Answer**

1. Wait for the dispatch notice: the tracking code is issued when the parcel is booked with SteadFast, Pathao, RedX, or Paperfly — not at checkout.
2. Find the code in your order confirmation message or on the customer dashboard under the order.
3. Enter it in the courier's tracking tool, or check the tracking console on your dashboard, which shows live delivery checkpoints.
4. For Cash on Delivery orders, the same tracking view confirms delivery before any payment dispute is raised.

**Conditions**

- No tracking code exists before dispatch; if your order still shows "Processing", the parcel has not been handed to the courier yet.
- Tracking updates depend on courier scans — a parcel with no scan for 48 hours is worth reporting to support with the order number.
- "Returned" status means the courier could not complete delivery (wrong address, recipient unavailable, or refused); support will arrange re-dispatch or a refund per the returns policy.
- Keep your order number even after delivery; it is required for any return, exchange, or refund request.
