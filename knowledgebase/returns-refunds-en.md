**Q: Can I return a product, and how do refunds work?**

Yes. Unused items in their original condition, with tags intact, can be returned or exchanged within 7 days of delivery. Full or line-level refunds are written back against the original payment record, so revenue reporting stays consistent.

**Answer**

1. Check the 7-day window: count from the day the parcel was delivered, not the order date.
2. Keep the item unused, unwashed, and in its original packaging with tags attached.
3. Start the request with your order number — through the support chat, phone, or email — and state whether you want an exchange or a refund.
4. Ship or hand over the return parcel as instructed by support.
5. Once the parcel is received and inspected, the refund is issued promptly to the original payment method or rail; COD orders are refunded to the customer-provided mobile-banking account.

**Conditions**

- Only items in original, unused condition qualify; used, damaged-by-customer, or tag-removed items are refused on inspection.
- The 7-day limit is strict; requests on day 8 or later are declined.
- Perishable goods, personal-care items with broken seals, and items marked final-sale at checkout are not returnable.
- Courier charges for the return trip may be deducted from the refund where the return is not caused by our error (wrong item, defective item, or damage in transit are always collected free).
