**Q: How do I reach a human, and when are you open?**

Two layers: the store you shop from, and the Framique platform behind it. Both answer in Bangla and English.

**Answer**

- This store: open daily 9:00 AM – 10:00 PM Bangladesh time, Saturday through Friday. Reach it via live chat, WhatsApp, or request an automated phone callback any time.
- Framique platform support: phone, email, and in-dashboard chat during published business hours, answered by people who work on the platform rather than a script-reading outsourced tier.
- Sales and migration: book a live demo of the builder, payments, and courier fulfilment from the contact page, or request a callback through the chat widget with your phone number and preferred time window (morning, afternoon, evening).
- Reliability: storefront, checkout, payment, and admin health are published live on the status page with the incident log — you hear it from the platform first, not from a customer.

**Conditions**

- Always include your order number (for store queries) or store name (for platform queries) so the right record is found first.
- Callbacks are scheduled in the requested window; same-working-day answers are the norm, not a guarantee.
- Anything involving another person's account or order cannot be discussed without verification — support will ask for the order details first.
