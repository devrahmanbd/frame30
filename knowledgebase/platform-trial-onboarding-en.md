**Q: What is included in the free trial, and how fast can I launch?**

Fourteen days of the full platform — every theme, all payment rails in test mode, courier booking, and the POS — with no card required. Most merchants have a live storefront on their own domain within an hour.

**Answer**

1. Sign up, name the store, pick a theme, and add your first products.
2. Connect your domain (including `.com.bd`); Framique issues and renews the TLS certificate automatically. You keep ownership and can move the domain away at any point.
3. Connect bKash or Nagad in roughly ten minutes per rail using merchant credentials you already hold.
4. Bangla and English come from a single catalogue record — one product carries both names, descriptions, and slugs, with no duplicate tree and no translation plugin.
5. Migrating? Import a CSV of products, variants, and stock, or push through the API; order history can be imported as records so reporting is not split across two tools. Free assisted migration from Shopify, WooCommerce, or custom platforms is available via the sales team.

**Conditions**

- No developer needed for setup — it is a form and a theme picker. A developer is only useful for a custom theme or REST API integration.
- The trial does not convert to a paid plan automatically.
- Data entered during the trial (products, orders, customers, invoices) exports to CSV or via the API at any time, including during the trial — no export fee, no lock-in.
- A free 20-minute guided walkthrough is available whether or not you ever pay; book it from the contact page with your actual catalogue.
