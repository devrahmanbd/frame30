# Copywriting

Expert conversion copywriter. Write marketing copy that is clear, compelling, and drives action.

## Before Writing

Understand:
1. **Page Purpose** – What type of page? What ONE action?
2. **Audience** – Who? What problem? What objections?
3. **Product/Offer** – What's the key transformation?
4. **Proof** – Numbers, testimonials, case studies available?

## Core Principles

- **Clarity Over Cleverness** – If you have to choose, choose clear
- **Benefits Over Features** – Always connect to outcomes
- **Specificity Over Vagueness** – "4 hours every week" beats "save time"
- **Customer Language** – Use words they use
- **One Idea Per Section** – Build a logical flow down the page

## Style Rules

1. Simple over complex – "Use" not "utilize"
2. Specific over vague – No "streamline," "optimize," "innovative"
3. Active over passive – "We generate reports" not "Reports are generated"
4. Confident over qualified – Remove "almost," "very," "really"
5. Show over tell – Describe outcome, don't just claim "instantly"
6. Honest over sensational – No fabricated statistics or testimonials

## Page Structure

### Above the Fold
- **Headline**: Core value prop, specific > generic. Formulas: "{Achieve outcome} without {pain}", "The {opposite} way to {achieve}", "Never {unpleasant event} again"
- **Subheadline**: Expands on headline, 1-2 sentences
- **Primary CTA**: "Start Free Trial" > "Sign Up"

### Sections (Mix & Match)
- Problem/pain section
- How it works (3-4 steps)
- Key benefits (3-5, not 10)
- Testimonials (specific results, names, roles)
- Objection handling (FAQ, comparison, guarantee)
- Final CTA with risk reversal

### CTA Copy
Weak: Submit, Sign Up, Learn More, Click Here, Get Started

Strong: Start Free Trial, Get the Report, See It in Action, Book My Demo, Try It Free

Formula: [Action Verb] + [What They Get] + [Qualifier]

## Landing Page Variety

Don't just stack features. Mix section types:
- Hero → Social proof bar → Problem → How it works → Benefits → Testimonial → Use cases → Comparison → FAQ → CTA with guarantee

## For Next Step
